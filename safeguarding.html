Safeguarding & Contact Procedure

For concerns connected with our volunteers, activities or community support.

Our commitment

Hope Freedom Ministry aims to treat children, adults at risk, beneficiaries, volunteers and community members with dignity and respect. Exploitation, abuse, harassment and discrimination are not acceptable.

Raise a concern

Share what happened, when and where it happened, who was involved and a safe way to contact you. Do not put yourself or another person at additional risk to gather evidence.

  • Email: hopenfreedomministry@gmail.com

Immediate danger

If someone is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical help, contact the relevant local emergency authority first. This website and WhatsApp number are not emergency services.

How concerns are handled

We will acknowledge the concern where contact details are provided, limit information to people who need it, and consider appropriate protective or referral steps. Confidentiality cannot be promised where disclosure is necessary to protect a person or meet a legal obligation.

Good-faith reporting

No person should face retaliation for raising a genuine concern in good faith. Deliberately false or malicious reports are not acceptable.
