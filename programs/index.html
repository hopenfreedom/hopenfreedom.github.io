Our operating principle

Support should protect dignity

  1. Listen to the person and understand the situation.
  2. Review need using responsible, privacy-conscious processes.
  3. Match support to available resources and program capacity.
  4. Protect children, women, and vulnerable adults through safeguarding practice.
  5. Report outcomes only when evidence is verified and consent is in place.
