Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Who we are

Hope Freedom Ministry operates in Yasham ki Bhattian, Lahore, Pakistan. This policy explains how we handle information shared through our website, email, WhatsApp and volunteer enquiries.

Information we may receive

We may receive your name, email address, telephone number and any information you choose to include in an enquiry. Our website does not directly collect card or bank details.

Donations

Online donations are completed on our official GoFundMe campaign. GoFundMe processes payment and personal information under its own privacy terms.

How information is used

  • To answer enquiries and support requests.
  • To coordinate volunteers and community assistance.
  • To keep records required for responsible ministry administration.

Third-party services

The website uses GitHub Pages for hosting and GTranslate for automatic translation. External links, including WhatsApp, Instagram and GoFundMe, are governed by those services’ privacy policies.

Retention and protection

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was provided and take proportionate steps to prevent unauthorised access.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information you previously shared, subject to any legitimate record-keeping need.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@hopefreedom.org or contact us on WhatsApp +92 309 7040468.
