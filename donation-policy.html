Donation Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Official online donation route

Hope Freedom Ministry currently accepts online donations only through its official GoFundMe campaign. The website does not collect card or bank details.

Use of donations

Donations support the ministry’s stated charitable activities, administration necessary to deliver those activities and the project needs described on this website or the campaign page.

Project allocation

Where a donation is associated with a particular appeal, we aim to use it for that purpose. If needs change or an appeal receives more than required, funds may be applied to a closely related or urgent ministry need.

Fees and receipts

Payment processing, transaction fees and receipts are handled by GoFundMe according to its terms. Donors should retain the confirmation issued by that platform.

Refunds

Refund requests are subject to GoFundMe’s processes and applicable requirements. Contact GoFundMe first and notify us if supporting information is needed.

No pressure

Giving is voluntary. No person should feel pressured to donate or expect personal benefit in return.

Questions

Before donating, you may contact us at hopenfreedomministry@gmail.com or WhatsApp +92 309 7040468.
